## Users

- Business Owners: register instruments, submit verification applications, track inspection progress, and access certificates.
- Legal Metrology Officers: review assigned instruments, complete field verification, record measurements and evidence, and submit inspection outcomes.
- Administrators: manage users, applications, assignments, certificates, compliance status, and enforcement actions.
- Public Users: validate the authenticity and current status of a certificate by scanning a QR code or entering a certificate ID.

## Product Purpose

e-VerifyMet is a digital Legal Metrology platform for managing the lifecycle of regulated weighing and measuring instruments in India. It connects instrument registration, verification applications, officer inspection workflows, digital certification, public verification, expiry monitoring, re-verification, and enforcement in a single trusted system.

Success means that regulated instruments can be tracked end-to-end with clear evidence, field workflows remain operational for officers, public users can verify certificate authenticity without a login, and administrative teams can maintain compliance reliably over time.

## Positioning

The product's differentiator is a unified digital instrument passport and lifecycle management model for regulated instruments. It combines registration, application processing, field verification, certificate issuance, QR verification, lifecycle tracking, and compliance oversight in one system rather than relying on fragmented manual records or isolated document workflows.

## Operating Context

- The product exists in the Indian legal metrology ecosystem and supports regulated instrument compliance workflows.
- Field verification is a core operating context for metrology officers and is expected to work effectively on mobile devices in real-world conditions.
- Public certificate verification is intended to be fast and simple without user accounts or complicated onboarding.
- Role-based access and evidence capture matter because the system is used by regulated businesses, authorized officers, and public-facing verification flows.
- The system is designed for operational trust, compliance clarity, and auditability, not for generic consumer-style engagement.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Manage instrument registration and lifecycle records.
- Support verification applications, scheduling, and assignment workflows.
- Enable field inspection with structured measurement and evidence capture.
- Issue and manage digital certificates with validity and expiry tracking.
- Support QR-based certificate verification and status lookups.
- Provide compliance monitoring for expiring or overdue instruments.
- Maintain audit trails for operational and enforcement actions.
- Distinguish clearly between business, officer, admin, and public user journeys.
- Keep the product responsive across mobile and desktop contexts, with strong emphasis on officer field use.
- Keep public verification simple and avoid exposing sensitive business information through the QR flow.
- Keep regulatory decisions in the hands of authorized officers rather than automatic or opaque adjudication.
- The system should remain extensible for future stakeholder types and regulatory workflows.

## Product Principles

1. Trust and authenticity are central to all verification and certificate flows.
2. The instrument is the core entity and should retain a persistent identity and lifecycle history.
3. Mobile-first field usability is non-negotiable for officers working in operational conditions.
4. Role-specific clarity and permission boundaries must remain explicit and easy to understand.
5. Government-grade clarity, accessibility, and operational trust matter more than decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- The system should remain touch-friendly, readable, and easy to use on mobile devices.
- Public verification should be intentionally simple and not require account creation or login.
- The experience should be clear and low-friction for users with varied digital literacy and device constraints.
- Design work should minimize ambiguity, support strong contrast and legibility, and prioritize operational clarity for real-world compliance tasks.
